# Communications Step by Step

Wiring
1. Mount the ear pieces and microphone in a racing helmet (most have pockets at the ears where it will fit nicely)
2. Mount the push to talk (PTT) button on the car where the driver desires, usually on the steering wheel (can be moved easily since the mount is velcro)
3. Connect the PTT harness to the radio, it should be a double aux jack (make sure this connection is inserted all the way)
4. When driver is in the car connect the helmet headset to the PTT harness

Radio Set up
1. Charge the radio batteries using the DC barrel plug on the side of the battery pack
2. Slide the battery into the back of the radio
3. Turn the volume knob on top to turn on the radio
4. Do this with the second radio as well
5. Set the channels of both radios to a UHF channel
   1. Press the settings cog
   2. Press the down or up arrows until you reach the desired channel

Radio Use
For the driver: press the PTT button to transmit
For the pit crew: press either the headset button or the transmit button on the side of the radio

The radios have an LED indicator

RED: transmitting

GREEN: recieving
