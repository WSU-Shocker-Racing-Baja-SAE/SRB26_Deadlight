# DAQ PCB Assembly

Each PCB section is labeled with the part that should be inserted and soldered at that section.

1. Solder male headers to the following modules: IMU, GPS, SD card breakout, thermocouple amplifier, and ESP32-S3.
2. Place each module's header pins into the PCB oriented to fit over its label and solder to the PCB
3. Place and solder the male 2 pin right angle JST-XH, male 6 pin right angle JST-XH, and male XT-30 connectors for the thermistor, display, and battery connection respectively
4. Place and solder the resistors and capacitor
5. Turn the PCB over
6. Trim the leads of the resistors and capacitor
7. With an 8 pin male header, cut off the top of every pin except the outer 2
8. Solder this to the voltage buck converter
9. Place and solder the buck converter to the back of the PCB

Tip: When soldering the XT-30 connector plug in the female connector so that the pins in the connector being soldered don't move
